========
Overview
========

This paragraph opens the document with no blank line above it, and this
one continues right after it, joined into a single paragraph.

A second paragraph runs right into the next block with no blank line.
* A bullet item right after with no blank line.
- A second bullet item.
+ A third bullet item.

More prose runs into the numbered list with no blank line.
1. A numbered item right after with no blank line.
2. Another numbered item, whose own text
   continues on a second line too.

A paragraph right before a line block, no blank line between them.
| A line block,
| its breaks kept.

A paragraph right before a comment, no blank line between them.
.. A bare comment line.

.. A comment with a body.
   Indented by exactly three spaces.
   And a second body line.

Trailing prose runs right into the next heading, no blank line needed.
Nested Section
--------------

A paragraph inside the nested section, joined across
two lines of its own.
